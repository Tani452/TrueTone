# TrueTone Enterprise Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines

1. **Brand Spelling:** Always format the brand name as **TrueTone** (CamelCase). Prohibit "truetone" or "True Tone".
2. **Probabilistic Framing (No Absolutes):** Prohibit absolute claims such as "100% infallible", "guarantees zero mistakes", "never fails", or "miracle".
   - *Approved Alternative:* "Engineered for 99.9% consistency across enterprise corpora".
3. **No Urgency Clickbait:** Prohibit retail hype phrases such as "ACT NOW", "HURRY", "CRAZY DEALS", or "DON'T MISS OUT".
   - *Approved Alternative:* "Enterprise volume tiers available through our customer portal".
4. **Professional Restraint:** Communicate with measured authority and dignity. Avoid sensationalism and limit exclamation marks.
