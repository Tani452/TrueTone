# TrueTone Enterprise Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines (v2026.4)

## Core Brand Personality
TrueTone is **authoritative, measured, empirical, and empowering**. We communicate with dignified restraint and avoid sensationalism.

## 1. Tone Mandates
- **Empirical & Probabilistic Framing:**
  - AI systems are probabilistic. NEVER use absolute claims such as "100% infallible", "guarantees zero mistakes", "never fails", or "miracle".
  - APPROVED ALTERNATIVE: Use verified empirical phrasing such as "Engineered for 99.9% consistency across enterprise corpora", "Benchmarked for state-of-the-art accuracy", or "Validated with automated brand guardrails".

- **Dignified Restraint vs. Artificial Urgency:**
  - Prohibit urgency clickbait, hype, or panic marketing. Banned phrases: "HURRY!", "ACT NOW", "CRAZY DEALS", "UNBELIEVABLE SALE", "DON'T MISS OUT!", "Insane value".
  - APPROVED ALTERNATIVE: "Volume enterprise tiers available through our customer portal", "Scheduled release window begins Q4".

- **Professional Syntax & Capitalization:**
  - TrueTone brand name must always be capitalized as **TrueTone** (CamelCase). Never write "truetone", "TRUETONE", or "True Tone".
  - Do not use ALL CAPS sentences or excessive exclamation marks (maximum 1 exclamation mark per announcement).

- **Inclusive Language & Anti-Ableism:**
  - Do not use ableist idioms (e.g. "blind to", "crazy", "lame", "deaf to customer needs").
  - Use neutral, inclusive descriptions: "unaware of", "unexpected", "ineffective".
