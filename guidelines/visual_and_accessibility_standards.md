# Visual & Asset Standards

1. **Approved Brand Color Palette:**
   - Deep Navy: `#0a0f1d`
   - Enterprise Slate: `#1e293b`
   - Electric Cyan: `#06b6d4`
   - Verified Emerald: `#10b981`
2. **Prohibited Visuals:**
   - Prohibit uncalibrated neon magenta (`#FF0055`) and hyper-saturated lime greens in official assets.
3. **Typography & Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA):**
   - Text overlays must maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against image backgrounds.
4. **Logo & Asset Integrity:**
   - Never stretch, skew, or alter the TrueTone logo emblem aspect ratio.
