# TrueTone Visual, Asset & Accessibility Standards (WCAG 2.2 AA)

## 1. Approved Brand Color Palette
- **Deep Navy Core:** `#0a0f1d` (Canvas background)
- **Enterprise Slate:** `#1e293b` (Cards, secondary surfaces)
- **Electric Cyan:** `#06b6d4` (Interactive focus, key accents)
- **Brand Cobalt:** `#3b82f6` (Primary interactive buttons)
- **Verified Emerald:** `#10b981` (Passing checks, status badges)
- **Warning Amber:** `#f59e0b` (Alerts, review notices)
- **Critical Rose:** `#ef4444` / `#dc2626` (Violations, errors)

### Prohibited Colors:
- Uncalibrated Neon Magenta (`#FF0055`, `#FF0077`), saturated lime greens, or harsh clashing high-saturation backgrounds are prohibited in official broadcast imagery.

## 2. Accessibility & Typography Contrast
- All text overlays on images, cards, and diagrams must maintain a minimum contrast ratio of **4.5:1** against the background (WCAG 2.2 AA).
- Do not place thin body text over dynamic un-scrimmed gradient imagery.
- When generating diagrams or infographics, ensure color is not the sole conveyor of information (use icons and clear text labels).

## 3. Logo & Visual Asset Consistency
- Logo Safe Zone: Maintain clear padding around the TrueTone emblem equal to half the icon's width.
- Never stretch, skew, distort aspect ratio, or recolor the TrueTone emblem in rainbow or unapproved gradients.
- Always use SVG or high-DPI PNG master assets from the official 2026 asset repository.
