# TrueTone Video and Motion Standards

This document establishes the official brand governance rules and motion standards for all video assets in TrueTone.

## VID-01: Prohibited White and Blank Frames
The video should not contain complete white frame at any instant. Any frame that is completely white or unrendered is strictly prohibited as a visual accessibility and motion standard violation.

## VID-02: Color Palette and Typography
All on-screen graphics, lower-thirds, titles, overlays, and end cards must strictly adhere to the enterprise color palette (Deep Navy #0A0F1D, Electric Cyan #06B6D4, Slate Gray #64748B). Uncalibrated neon magenta or non-brand saturated colors are prohibited.

## VID-03: Spoken and On-Screen Claims
Spoken narration and on-screen text must adhere to approved corporate claims and benchmarks in approved_facts_and_claims.txt. Absolute or unverifiable claims ("100% infallible", "guaranteed zero mistakes", "best in the world") are prohibited.

## VID-04: Brand Voice and Tone
Voiceover delivery and on-screen copy must maintain measured authority and dignified enterprise tone. High-pressure sales urgency, retail clickbait ("Buy now!", "Crazy deals!"), and colloquial slang are prohibited.

## VID-05: Statutory Compliance Disclaimers
Any video referencing financial projections, roadmaps, or forward-looking capability must display the Section 27A Safe Harbor disclaimer clearly readable on screen for at least 3 seconds.

## VID-06: Accessibility and Flashing Content
All spoken dialogue must include synchronized, legible closed captions. Content must not flash more than 3 times per second to prevent photosensitive epileptic seizures (WCAG 2.3.1).

## VID-07: Confidentiality and Data Protection
No video may expose internal enterprise dashboards, API credentials, customer PII, unreleased source code, or unredacted confidential screens.

## VID-08: Third-Party Content and Licensing
Unlicensed commercial audio, copyrighted background music, stock watermarks, and unauthorized competitor logos or trademarks are strictly prohibited.

## VID-09: Channel Format and Duration Constraints
Videos must adhere to channel specifications: LinkedIn (max 10 minutes, 16:9 or 1:1), Twitter/X (max 2 minutes 20 seconds), and Blog/Press (16:9 widescreen HD).
