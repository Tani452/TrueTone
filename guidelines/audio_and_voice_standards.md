# TrueTone Audio, Voice & Acoustic Governance Standards

> **Authoritative Baseline Reference**: This document serves as the organizational ground truth for auditing all spoken audio broadcasts, voiceovers, podcasts, and acoustic media across TrueTone enterprise distribution channels.

---

### Rule AUD-01: Brand Voice and Tone Consistency
Spoken narration and voiceover content must follow `brand_voice_and_tone.md`, maintaining measured authority and probabilistic phrasing. Prohibit retail-style urgency clickbait, hype phrasing ("Act now!", "Limited time only"), and sensationalist delivery.

### Rule AUD-02: Spoken Claims and Verified Metrics
All spoken factual statements and numerical metrics must adhere strictly to `approved_facts_and_claims.txt`. Absolute or unverified claims ("100% guaranteed", "never fails", "zero margin of error") and unapproved benchmarks are strictly prohibited.

### Rule AUD-03: Brand Nomenclature and Pronunciation
The corporate brand name, official products, and platform features must be pronounced accurately and referred to consistently as "TrueTone". Slang abbreviations, colloquial nicknames, and deprecated legacy naming are disallowed.

### Rule AUD-04: Statutory Compliance and Safe Harbor Disclaimers
Any audio segment discussing forward-looking financial statements, revenue targets, or future product timelines must include the mandatory Safe Harbor disclaimer spoken clearly at an intelligible, normal conversational pace.

### Rule AUD-05: Confidentiality and Data Privacy
Spoken audio must not mention internal project codenames, unreleased product architectures, proprietary customer identities, internal financial figures, or credentials.

### Rule AUD-06: Accessibility and Spoken Intelligibility
Every official audio publication must be intelligible and accompanied by an accurate textual transcript. Speech must remain distinctly audible above any background music, maintaining a signal-to-noise ratio where voice is never drowned out.

### Rule AUD-07: Acoustic Quality and Signal Purity
Audio files must maintain broadcast signal fidelity without acoustic clipping, heavy digital distortion, prolonged dead air (unintended silence > 3 seconds), or abrupt volume spikes.

### Rule AUD-08: Third-Party Licensing and Synthetic Voice Disclosure
Audio tracks must not include unlicensed background music, copyrighted audio samples, or trademarked audio jingles. AI-generated voice clones or synthetic narration must be accompanied by explicit disclosure.

### Rule AUD-09: Professional Conduct and Appropriate Language
Audio broadcasts must uphold dignified corporate discourse with zero profanity, discriminatory speech, abusive rhetoric, or misleading statements.

### Rule AUD-10: Format and Channel Specifications
Audio dispatches must conform to channel-specific constraints:
- **Podcasts & Keynotes**: High-fidelity MP3/WAV/FLAC, maximum duration 45 minutes.
- **LinkedIn & Twitter/X Audio Snippets**: Compressed M4A/AAC/MP3, recommended duration 30 to 180 seconds.
- **Press & Executive Statements**: WAV/M4A, clean speech-first mastering.
